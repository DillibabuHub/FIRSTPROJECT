# who I am

I'm a frontend developer who likes turning plain HTML and CSS into interfaces that feel considered — not templated. I care about semantic markup, readable code, and interactions that respond the moment you touch them.

I studied Computer Applications at Anna University, Chennai, and spent the last two years building interfaces for small product teams — mostly dashboards, marketing pages, and the occasional weird side project that never should have worked but did.

# how I work

No frameworks unless the project earns them. I'd rather ship 40kb of vanilla JS that loads instantly than 400kb of abstraction nobody asked for. Accessibility and keyboard focus aren't an afterthought — they're part of the first draft.
